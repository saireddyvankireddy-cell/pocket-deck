      Known limitations
        Use away from home

        The app needs an always-on cloud server to work when your Mac is off. Hosting and an HTTPS address have not been set up yet. Your pCloud account keeps the originals and saved app records.

        HTTPS enables home-screen installation and offline app caching where Safari supports it. It does not guarantee permanent device storage or background execution.

        Delete selected photos?

          This removes the selected originals from pCloud and moves them to pCloud Trash. Review visually matching or similar photos before deleting. Every selected photo will be deleted, including the largest copy if you selected it.

          Add song to playlist

          Playlist names and songs are saved in your pCloud app folder. A connection and write permission are required.

          Connect pCloud with a token

          Use an existing pCloud token. Its permissions still apply: a read-only token cannot delete files. This is not your app’s client secret.

          Delete this song from pCloud?

          This deletes the original song from pCloud and removes it from your liked songs, recent history, playlists and device cache. Other devices need to refresh. Recovery is through pCloud Trash within your account’s retention period.

          Delete album?

          Photos and videos stay safely in pCloud unless you tick the option below.

          Delete this photo from pCloud?

          This deletes the original from your pCloud account. Other devices show the change after refreshing their libraries. Recovery is through pCloud Trash, within your account’s retention period.
